Our Craft

HOW WE DO IT.

Every pizza at Fuoco follows the same four-step ritual. No shortcuts allowed.

01
THE DOUGH
Imported Caputo 00 flour, minimal yeast, 48-hour cold fermentation. Pillowy, charred, unforgettable.
02
THE SAUCE
San Marzano DOP tomatoes crushed by hand. Salt only. No cooking — the oven does the rest.
03
THE FIRE
Oak wood, 450°C, 90 seconds. The leopard-spotted char you see on the crust? That's the goal.
04
YOUR TABLE
From oven to your hands in under two minutes. Eat it hot. Eat it fast. That's the rule.
